## Before connecting hardware

Match the controller revision, firmware target, drive configuration, motors and encoders to your robot. Use revision-specific electrical drawings and verify voltage/pinout before connection. Commission with controlled motor movement and emergency-stop access; software zero velocity is not physical power isolation.
