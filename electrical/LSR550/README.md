The PDFs preserve the provided wiring, voltage labels, component images, arrows and limitations. Commercial logos and QR codes were removed from illustrations; no circuit connections or technical text were changed. These are illustrated reference layouts, not a package bill of materials or an as-built diagram for every LSR550. Confirm the actual controller, driver, computer, sensors and power arrangement before connection. The drawings explicitly note that alternative computers may require different power wiring and that the illustrated LiDAR may be replaced.

The labels “Hardware E-stop” and “Software E-stop” identify the depicted controls; they do not establish power-isolation boundaries, braking mode, stopping distance or firmware timeout. These LSR550 drawings must not be used as LSR550A wiring diagrams or complete robotic-arm wiring diagrams.
